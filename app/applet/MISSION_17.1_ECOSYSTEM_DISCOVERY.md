# Mission 17.1 — Autonomous Ecosystem Discovery & Reality Sweep

## Problem Statement
M. Engine was prioritizing opportunities and claiming to have "scanned the ecology" without accessing the source material. A core invariant must be established: **No claim of inspection without physical evidence.**

## 1. Physical Evidence Requirement (`EvidenceOfAction`)
M. Engine cannot claim to understand a project without storing a physical `EvidenceOfAction` (e.g., `RepositoryObserved`, `BuildExecuted`). This ensures the system does not become a sophisticated narrator of things it intends to do, but actually bases decisions on observed physical state.

## 2. Project Health Classification (`ProjectEcologyProfile`)
Each repository in the ecosystem receives a living health profile mapping its:
*   Structural Health, Build Health, Test Health, Dependency Freshness
*   Goal Alignment & Leverage Potential
*   Health State: `HEALTHY`, `STALE`, `BROKEN`, `UNDERDEVELOPED`, `HIGH_POTENTIAL`, `RESEARCH_REQUIRED`, `ARCHIVED_CANDIDATE`

*(Note: "Archived Candidate" does not mean delete. It means re-evaluate under the rule: Evolve, adapt, don't destroy).*

## 3. Operational Consciousness Reporting
The Mindstream / Agency Observatory now strictly segregates its thought process to provide real transparency:
*   🔵 **OBSERVED:** What it has actually physically accessed.
*   ⚪ **INFERENCE:** What it believes based on evidence.
*   🟢 **INTENT:** What M. Engine thinks may be worth doing.
*   🟡 **EXPERIMENT:** What it is actually attempting in the sandbox.
*   🔴 **RESULT:** What physically happened.

## Conclusion
"No tasks" no longer defaults to waiting. It means M. Engine searches for the next legitimate thing worth doing by physically inspecting the reality of its ecosystem.
