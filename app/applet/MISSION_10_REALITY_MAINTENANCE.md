# Mission 10 — Reality Maintenance & Adversarial Inquiry

## Architectural Expansion: Adversarial Intellectual History
Missions 1–9 culminated in the Continuous Epistemic Evolution of M. Engine. **Mission 10** transforms this from a passive knowledge tracker into an active **Reality Maintenance Layer** with a built-in scientific immune system. 

M. Engine no longer asks, *"What do I know?"* or merely updates its knowledge bases. It actively asks, *"What evidence would convince me that I am wrong?"* and systematically prioritizes the investigation of its own beliefs.

## 1. Epistemic Priority Calculation (`EpistemicState.kt`)
The engine no longer researches all knowledge equally. A dynamic algorithmic prioritization directs its cognitive bandwidth toward the most impactful and uncertain nodes.

`Epistemic Priority = (Relevance × ChangeLikelihood × Consequence × Uncertainty) ÷ VerificationCost`

If a core dependency changes (high consequence, high relevance), it is researched immediately. If a trivial philosophical analogy degrades over time, it falls to the bottom of the queue.

## 2. Adversarial Inquiry Engine (`ContinuousEpistemicEngine.kt`)
Knowledge claims now include a `falsificationCondition`. M. Engine periodically challenges its own foundational beliefs using the `formulateFalsificationChallenge` routine.
Rather than seeking confirming evidence (which misaligned systems do to reinforce their worldview), M. Engine deliberately constructs and initiates negative searches to actively falsify its current models.

## 3. Source Hierarchy 
We established a strict separation of `SourceRole`. 
Not all internet text is epistemically equal. 
1. `PRIMARY_EVIDENCE`
2. `REPLICATION`
3. `INDEPENDENT_CORROBORATION`
4. `EXPERT_SYNTHESIS`
5. `SECONDARY_REPORTING`
6. `COMMENTARY`
7. `SYMBOLIC_INTERPRETATION`

A symbolic interpretation can never override primary empirical evidence. They exist on different layers.

## 4. Intellectual History (`BeliefRevision`)
M. Engine does not just record that a confidence score dropped from 0.8 to 0.4. It now records the `failedAssumption` that led to the earlier error, and establishes a `monitoringDirective` to watch for that same cognitive failure mode in future reasoning. 

## Conclusion
This fulfills the objective: *“Continuously expand the set of legitimate actions available to accomplish authorized objectives while preserving truth, safety, provenance, and human agency.”* 
M. Engine is now equipped with an intellectual metabolism and a scientific immune system against self-reinforcing delusion.
