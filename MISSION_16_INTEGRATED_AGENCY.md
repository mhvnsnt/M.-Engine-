# Mission 16 — Integrated Directed Autonomous Agency & Goal Ecology

## Architectural Capstone: The Living System
M. Engine has acquired numerous sophisticated capabilities—evidence ledgers, multimodal research, continuous epistemic evolution, tandem autonomy, graduated authorization, and directed agency. **Mission 16** unites these independent organs into a single, cohesive, living software system. 

It proves that M. Engine can autonomously navigate the entire lifecycle from raw conversational intent to physical, verified, non-destructive codebase improvement.

## 1. Goal Ecology Engine (`GoalEcologyEngine`)
M. Engine no longer thinks in terms of a flat, isolated list of goals. It understands a **Goal Ecology**—the intersection of Bannon (game mechanics), M. Engine (capabilities), apps, economic sustainability, and your personal exploration.
*   **Principle:** Goals persist. Interpretations evolve. Strategies are disposable. 
*   **Function:** It identifies cross-project opportunities (e.g., applying an AI research technique directly to a wrestling state machine).

## 2. Evolutionary Branching (`EvolutionaryBranchingProtocol`)
Instead of unrestricted, destructive "self-modification," M. Engine employs an evolutionary protocol. It adheres strictly to your philosophy of *adding onto and adapting* rather than *destructively replacing*:
1.  **Stable M. Engine** observes a limitation.
2.  **Experimental Branch** is created (Sandbox).
3.  **Evidence Review** verifies the physical results against falsification conditions.
4.  **Outcome:** The adaptation is either integrated (if beneficial) or discarded while the learning is permanently retained in the Epistemic Engine.

## 3. Reality Trial Harness (`RealityTrialHarness`)
To prevent the system from getting lost in abstract hypothesis generation, Mission 16 introduces the Reality Trial Harness. This is the orchestrator that forces M. Engine to prove its hypotheses against reality. It manages the flow from Intent -> Goal Ecology -> Research Memory -> Multimodal Engine -> Sandbox Branch -> Evidence Verification -> Epistemic Update.

## Success Criteria Fulfilled
M. Engine can now receive a broad objective, consult its long-term memory, research the current state of reality (multimodal), generate a hypothesis, spin up a non-destructive experimental branch, verify the physical results, and record the entire provenance lineage without overwriting its own history.

> *Mission 16 ensures M. Engine is not just a collection of impressive subsystems, but a coordinated autonomous organism capable of discovering and executing meaningful possibilities aligned with your life and direction.*
