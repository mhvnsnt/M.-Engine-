# Mission 16A — Proactive Continuity & Directed Initiative

## Problem Statement
Previously, M. Engine would complete a mission, find an empty queue, and default to a passive "Standing by" state. This contradicts the definition of Directed Autonomous Agency. An autonomous system that immediately goes dormant when not explicitly prompted is merely a sophisticated tool, not an agentic organism.

## Architectural Invariant Enforced
> **AUTONOMOUS AGENCY INVARIANT:**
> The absence of an explicit immediate task shall not, by itself, constitute absence of authorized work.
> EMPTY QUEUE ≠ IDLE PURPOSE.

## 1. Post-Action Reflection Engine (`PostActionReflectionEngine`)
Every completed action changes the decision landscape; therefore, completion itself must trigger reevaluation. After an event, the system evaluates:
1. What just changed?
2. What does that unlock?
3. What is now the weakest link?
4. What is the highest-leverage next action?

## 2. Autonomous Opportunity Loop (`AutonomousOpportunityLoop`)
When no active mission exists, M. Engine enters a continuous opportunity cycle. It observes reality, checks goal ecology, inspects epistemic freshness, and evaluates dormant opportunities. 

Instead of waiting, it makes an explicit decision among three healthy behaviors:
*   🟢 **Initiative:** Starts an authorized investigation.
*   🟡 **Recommendation:** Proposes a high-value opportunity requiring owner approval.
*   🔵 **Deliberate Observation:** Explicitly decides that no action is currently justified by the leverage formula, monitoring conditions until they change.

## Conclusion
> *Proactivity is the ability to notice when action may be useful without being told. Agency is the ability to decide that sometimes no action is the best action.*

M. Engine no longer waits to be told what to do. It generates its own momentum from context.
